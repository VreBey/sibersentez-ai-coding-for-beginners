The code of SiberSentez is free software (GNU GPL version 3 or later; the kit under the MIT License). The name
**SiberSentez** and the SiberSentez logos are not part of those licenses: they say who made a copy, so people can
trust what they install.

## You may

- Say that your project works with, was built with or is based on SiberSentez ("based on SiberSentez", "a fork of
  SiberSentez").
- Use the name to talk about SiberSentez: in articles, videos, reviews, courses and links to
  [sibersentez.com](https://sibersentez.com).
- Share unchanged copies of the official installer and keep its name and logo.

## You may not, without written permission

- Publish a changed version, a fork or a product under the name SiberSentez or with its logo. Give it its own name
  and icon, and say what it is based on.
- Use the name or the logo in a way that suggests the SiberSentez project made, checked or supports your product or
  service.
- Use the name or a similar name in a domain, an app store entry or a company name for software of this kind.

The licenses keep all the rights they give you over the code; this page only covers the name and the logo.
